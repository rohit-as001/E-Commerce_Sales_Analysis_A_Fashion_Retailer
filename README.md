# E-Commerce_Sales_Analysis_A_Fashion_Retailer
### This Repository has E-Commerce Sales Analysis projects.



# Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the sales performance of a fashion e-commerce business using an interactive dashboard.
The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales trends, customer demographics, order behavior, and channel performance, enabling stakeholders to make data-driven decisions.

It allows dynamic filtering by Gender and Age Group, helping uncover deeper customer insights.


## Key KPIs
- Total Sales: 21.2M
- Total Quantity: 31.2K
- Top State: Maharashtra (2.99M)
- Top City: Bengaluru (1.77M)



# Key Insights

1) Sales Channel Performance

- Ajio (36%) and Myntra (23%) are the leading sales channels.
- Amazon and Flipkart also contribute significantly.
- Smaller platforms like Nykaa and Others have minimal contribution.


2) Monthly Sales Trend

  - Peak sales observed in March (1.93M).
  - Gradual decline towards year-end (Nov–Dec).
  - Indicates strong seasonal demand in early months.
  
3) B2C vs B2B Analysis

  - B2C dominates sales (~21M), accounting for almost the entire revenue.
  - B2B contribution is very low, indicating untapped business opportunities.

4) Order Status Insights

  - 93% orders are successfully delivered, showing strong operational efficiency.
  - Very low percentage of returns, cancellations, and refunds, which is a positive indicator.

5️) Category Performance

  - Set category (~10.5M) dominates sales significantly.
  - Followed by Kurta and Western Dress.
  - Categories like Footwear and Jeans contribute very little.

6️) Customer Demographics

  - Adults (12.5K customers) are the largest customer group.
  - Followed by Middle-aged adults.
  - Senior segment has the lowest participation.


## Business Recommendations

1. Strengthen High-Performing Channels
  - Increase investment in Ajio and Myntra through exclusive deals and promotions.
  - Optimize listings and ads on Amazon and Flipkart for better visibility.

2. Expand B2B Opportunities
  - Develop partnerships with retailers and bulk buyers.
  - Introduce bulk discounts and corporate sales programs.

3. Seasonal Sales Optimization
  - Maximize inventory and marketing during peak months (Jan–Mar).
  - Run campaigns and discounts during low-performing months (Nov–Dec).

4. Product Strategy Improvement
  - Focus on high-demand categories like Set and Kurta.
  - Improve or reposition low-performing categories like Footwear and Jeans.

5. Customer Segmentation Strategy
  - Target Adult and Middle-aged groups with personalized campaigns.
  - Introduce offers to attract younger and senior segments.
